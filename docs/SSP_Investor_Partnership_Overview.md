# SSP First-Position Joint Venture Partnership
## For Accredited Investors Only

---

## Structure

SSP structures deal-by-deal joint venture partnerships on foreclosure and bank REO residential real estate flips.

**Key Points:**
- Deal-by-deal joint ventures (not a fund, not pooled capital, not passive syndication)
- Investor funds the full purchase price of individual properties (typically $175k–$220k)
- SSP advances rehab and holding costs
- Profits are split 50/50 at sale
- Investor capital is positioned in first position via joint venture agreement
- No fees. No preferred return. No profit until exit.

---

## Capital Flow

**Investor Capital:**
- Investor provides full purchase price ($175k–$220k typical)
- Capital flows to licensed title company at closing
- Title company holds funds in escrow
- Investor capital secured in first position via joint venture agreement

**SSP Capital:**
- SSP advances rehab costs (typically $20k–$40k)
- SSP covers holding costs (taxes, insurance, utilities)
- SSP capital is subordinated to investor capital

**Sale Proceeds:**
- Sale proceeds flow through title company
- Title company distributes:
  - Investor receives original capital + 50% of profit
  - SSP receives 50% of profit
- No funds flow directly to SSP operating accounts

---

## Responsibilities

**Investor:**
- Provides purchase capital (full purchase price per property)
- Receives 50% of profit at sale
- Capital secured in first position

**SSP:**
- Sources undervalued foreclosure and REO properties
- Structures joint venture agreements
- Manages renovation process
- Handles property sale
- Advances rehab and holding costs
- Receives 50% of profit at sale

---

## Typical Timeline & Returns

**Hold Period:**
- Typical: 3–6 months from purchase to sale
- Renovation: 4–8 weeks
- Listing to sale: 30–60 days

**Profit Range:**
- Typical profit per deal: $30k–$60k (varies by property)
- Investor share (50%): $15k–$30k per deal
- Actual results vary by execution and market conditions

**Timeline Breakdown:**
1. Purchase → Closing (investor capital deployed)
2. Renovation → 4–8 weeks (SSP manages)
3. List → Property listed for sale
4. Sale → 30–60 days average
5. Exit → Profit distribution at closing
