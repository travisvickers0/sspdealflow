# SSP First-Position Joint Venture Partnerships
## For Accredited Investors Only

*Deal-by-deal real estate partnerships backed by residential assets*

---

## SECTION 1 — WHAT THIS IS

Southern Specialty Properties LLC partners with accredited investors on a deal-by-deal, first-position joint venture basis to acquire, renovate, and sell residential foreclosure and bank-owned (REO) properties.

**This is not a fund.**
**There is no pooled capital.**
**Each property stands on its own.**

---

## SECTION 2 — PARTNERSHIP STRUCTURE

**Investment Type**
First-position joint venture on an individual residential property

**Typical Capital Per Deal**
$175,000–$220,000 (full purchase price only)

**Use of Investor Capital**
Investor funds are wired directly to the licensed title company or closing attorney to acquire the property.
SSP does not take custody of investor funds.

**Title Holder**
Southern Specialty Properties LLC

**SSP Capital Position**
Subordinated to investor purchase capital

**Profit Split**
50% Investor / 50% SSP

**Payout Timing**
At sale of the property

---

## SECTION 3 — CAPITAL FLOW (SIMPLIFIED)

1. Investor capital → Title company (first position)

2. SSP capital → Subordinated (rehab + holding costs)

3. Property sold

4. Sale proceeds → Title company

5. Capital returned → Profits split 50/50

All funds move through the title company. No transfers to SSP operating accounts.

---

## SECTION 4 — ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

### What Investors Do

- Fund the full purchase price at closing

- Review individual opportunities

- Receive capital back plus profit share at sale

### What SSP Does

- Source and underwrite foreclosure and REO properties

- Negotiate acquisition and manage closing

- Advance rehab and holding costs

- Manage renovations, listing, and sale

- Provide a detailed closing and payout summary

---

## SECTION 5 — TYPICAL TIMELINE

1. Acquisition & closing

2. Renovation and prep (30–60 days typical)

3. Listing and sale (varies by market)

Most projects target a 60–120 day total hold, though timelines vary.

---
