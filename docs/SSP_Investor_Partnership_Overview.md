# SSP Investor Partnership Overview
## SSP First-Position Joint Venture Partnerships

### For Accredited Investors Only
*Deal-by-deal real estate partnerships backed by residential assets*

---

## SECTION 1 — OVERVIEW

Southern Specialty Properties LLC partners with accredited investors on a deal-by-deal, first-position joint venture basis to acquire, renovate, and sell residential foreclosure and bank-owned (REO) properties.

**This is not a fund.**

**There is no pooled capital.**

**Each investment is structured and evaluated independently.**

---

## SECTION 2 — PARTNERSHIP STRUCTURE

**Investment Type**
First-position joint venture on an individual residential property

**Typical Capital Per Deal**
$175,000–$220,000 (purchase price only)

**Use of Investor Capital**
Investor funds are wired directly to the licensed title company or closing attorney for property acquisition.
Southern Specialty Properties does not take custody of investor funds.

**Title Holder**
Southern Specialty Properties LLC or a property-specific affiliated entity

**SSP Capital Position**
Subordinated to investor purchase capital

**Profit Allocation**
50% Investor / 50% SSP

**Distribution Timing**
Upon sale of the property

---

## SECTION 3 — CAPITAL FLOW (SIMPLIFIED)

• Investor capital → Title company (first position)
• SSP capital → Subordinated (rehab and holding costs)
• Property sold
• Sale proceeds → Title company
• Capital returned → Remaining profits split 50/50

All funds move through the title company. No investor capital is transferred to SSP operating accounts.

---

## SECTION 4 — ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

**Investor Responsibilities**
• Fund the full purchase price at closing
• Review and approve individual investment opportunities
• Receive return of capital and profit participation upon sale

**SSP Responsibilities**
• Source and underwrite foreclosure and REO opportunities
• Negotiate acquisitions and manage closings
• Advance and manage renovation and holding costs
• Oversee rehabilitation, listing, and disposition
• Provide a detailed closing and distribution summary

---

## SECTION 5 — TYPICAL TIMELINE

• Acquisition and closing
• Renovation and preparation (typically 30–60 days)
• Listing and sale (market dependent)

Most projects target a 60–120 day total hold period, though timelines may vary.

---

## SECTION 6 — IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES

• Accredited investors only
• Not a fund or pooled investment vehicle
• No guarantees or promised returns
• Investments involve risk, including potential loss of capital
• Past performance does not guarantee future results
