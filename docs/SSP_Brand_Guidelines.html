Section 01

Brand identity

Confident. Premium.
Numbers-forward.

SSP Deal Flow is a JV investment platform for accredited investors. The brand feels like a luxury real estate brochure on marketing pages, and a private investor dashboard on deal surfaces.

Voice

Short. Declarative. Transparent. Lead with numbers, not hype. Avoid buzzwords, emojis, and salesy superlatives.

“Vetted off-market acquisitions. 50/50 profit split at sale. No fees, no pooled capital.”

Visual mood

Two coexisting aesthetics — never mixed on the same surface:

  • Cream / editorial — Home, Track Record, How It Works, Contact
  • Dark luxury — Property Detail, Invest, Qualify, dialogs

Cream / editorial

Real estate
built for
investors

Warm off-white, ink type, accent red for emphasis. Brochure energy.

Dark luxury

Deal terms
that hold up

Near-black, cream type, red CTAs. Private investor dashboard.

Logo

Wordmark & lockups

The logo is set in Bebas Neue, all caps, with tracking 0.08em. Brackets are accent red and slightly larger than the wordmark.

On cream

[SSP DEAL FLOW]

On dark

Construction

Do

  • Use Bebas Neue only for the logo
  • Keep brackets in accent red #e8432d
  • Wordmark: ink on cream, cream on dark
  • Nav size ~22px word / 28px brackets

Don't

  • Stretch, outline, or add drop shadows
  • Recolor brackets to black or white
  • Set the wordmark in sentence case
  • Place on busy photography without a clear panel
