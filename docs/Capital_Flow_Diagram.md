## Design Rules

### Color Coding
- **Investor Capital:** Green (#10B981 or similar)
- **Title Company:** Blue (#3B82F6 or similar) - Most prominent
- **SSP Capital:** Gray (#6B7280 or similar) - Subdued
- **Sale Proceeds:** Amber (#F59E0B or similar)

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Title Company** - Largest box, central position, most prominent
2. **Investor Capital** - Second largest, green color, solid arrows
3. **SSP Capital** - Smaller box, gray color, dashed arrows
4. **Sale Proceeds** - Medium size, amber color

### Arrow Styles
- **Investor to Title Company:** Solid, thick, green
- **SSP to Title Company:** Dashed, thin, gray
- **Sale to Title Company:** Solid, medium, amber
- **Title Company Distribution:** Two equal arrows, one green (investor), one gray (SSP)

### Key Visual Elements to Emphasize
1. **No arrows pointing to SSP operating accounts** - Critical
2. **Title company is central control point** - Emphasize with size and position
3. **Investor capital visually larger/more prominent** - Shows first position
4. **SSP capital clearly subordinated** - Smaller, dashed lines, gray color

## Notes for Designer

1. **Do NOT include:**
   - Arrows pointing to SSP operating accounts
   - Pooled capital imagery
   - Fund structure visuals
   - Any suggestion of fees or preferred returns

2. **DO emphasize:**
   - Title company as central control
   - Investor capital in first position
   - Clear separation of investor and SSP capital
   - Simple, linear flow

3. **Tone:**
   - Institutional, clean, professional
   - No marketing language
   - Structure-focused, not sales-focused

4. **Layout Options:**
   - Vertical flow (top to bottom) recommended
   - Or horizontal flow (left to right) if space allows
   - Ensure title company is visually central in either layout
