Here is exactly where to bump the size:

1. The Headline (Go Massive)
Currently, your headline is polite. You want it to be confident.

The Change: Increase the font size of "Real Estate Opportunities" and "Built for Investors."

Why: In modern high-end web design, the headline often takes up 50% of the screen width. It anchors the page.

Tailwind Tweak:

JavaScript

{/* Current looks like text-4xl or 5xl */}
<h1 className="text-5xl md:text-7xl font-extrabold tracking-tight text-gray-900 leading-tight">
  Real Estate Opportunities <br/>
  <span className="text-red-600">Built for Investors</span>
</h1>
Note: Adding tracking-tight (letter-spacing) often makes huge text look more premium.

2. The Stat Numbers (The "Hero" Data)
Your stats container looks great, but the numbers (29, 94d, $3.4M) are the main reason someone will trust you. They should be unmissable.

The Change: Bump the numbers up one or two steps.

Tailwind Tweak: Change them from text-3xl (or 4xl) to text-5xl.

JavaScript

<p className="text-5xl font-extrabold text-gray-900">29</p>
3. The Feature List (Checkmarks)
The text next to the checkmarks ("10+ Years Verified Exits", etc.) is a little too small to read comfortably at a glance.

The Change: Bump these from text-sm (approx 14px) to text-base (16px) or text-lg (18px).

Why: These are your key selling points. You don't want users to squint to read them.

Summary Checklist for "Pop"
[ ] Headline: text-6xl or text-7xl (Make it huge).

[ ] Stat Numbers: text-5xl (Make them dominant).

[ ] Stat Labels: Keep them small (text-xs), but make them bolder (font-bold) so they are legible without stealing attention from the numbers.

[ ] Primary Button: Make the text inside "Explore Properties" slightly larger (text-lg) so the button feels more substantial.

One final tiny polish: On your "Accredited Investors Only" pill, try adding tracking-widest to the class list. Spacing out the letters (K E R N I N G) on small uppercase text is a classic designer trick to make it look expensive.
