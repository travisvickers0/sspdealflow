Redesign the homepage hero section using the SAME CONTENT but with a premium Apple × Airbnb × modern fintech aesthetic. This hero should feel calm, institutional, and confident, not salesy. It should look like a private investor platform rather than a marketing funnel.

Design principles to follow:
Use generous whitespace and restrained hierarchy. No loud gradients, no heavy borders, no badges or hype language. Typography, spacing, and subtle motion should do the work. The result should feel quietly confident and trustworthy.

Hero container:
Center the content with a max width around 1200px and large vertical padding. Use a warm off-white or light stone background. Optional very subtle noise texture for depth. Mobile-first and fully responsive.

Top context line:
Place a small, muted line above the headline that reads “Accredited Investors Only”. Style it in light gray with a subtle shield or lock icon. This should NOT be a badge or pill, just quiet contextual text.

Headline:
Use a two-line, sentence-case headline with modern humanist sans typography:
“Real estate opportunities
built for disciplined investors”
Keep the font weight medium, not bold. Limit line width to roughly 650–700px. Emphasize “disciplined investors” using slightly stronger font weight only, not color.

Subheadline:
Place directly beneath the headline:
“Deal-by-deal joint ventures focused on capital protection, clean structures, and repeatable execution.”
Use softer gray text, larger than body copy but clearly secondary to the headline. Max width around 720px.

Proof points:
Display three text columns with subtle dividers, no icons or checkmarks:
• 10+ years foreclosure and REO exits nationwide
• 50/50 profit split at sale
• No management or platform fees
This should feel like a spec sheet, not marketing copy. Stack vertically on mobile.

Metrics bar:
Display four metrics in a single horizontal row with thin dividers and no cards:
29 — Deals Closed
94d — Avg Hold
$3.4M — Total Equity
250+ — Active Investors
Numbers should be slightly larger than labels. Labels muted. Include a very subtle count-up animation on load.

Calls to action:
Primary CTA:
“Explore Current Opportunities →”
Use a rounded rectangle (not a pill), dark charcoal or soft black background, white text, subtle hover lift and shadow. Airbnb-style primary button.

Secondary CTA:
“How the Partnership Works”
Text-only button beneath or beside the primary CTA. Muted color. Underline appears on hover. No border.

Motion:
Use subtle fade-up animations (6–8px translate) for the headline, subheadline, and metrics. No bounce, no spring effects.

Technical requirements:
Implement using React and TailwindCSS. Clean JSX structure. Componentized hero. No hard-coded pixel values unless necessary. Mobile-first responsive behavior. Desktop content left-aligned, mobile content centered. Optional dark-mode support.

Overall result:
The hero should feel premium, restrained, and designed for serious investors. It should signal discipline, repeatability, and trust without selling or overselling anything.
